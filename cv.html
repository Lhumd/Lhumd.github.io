Education

Experience

  • Jun – Nov 2025 Visiting Researcher, Technical University of Munich, Germany, supported by Majid Khadiv.
    Proposed SLowRL and fine-tuned a jumping policy on a physical Unitree Go2; developed SafeExplorer.
  • 2019 – 2021 Robotics Researcher and Research Intern, Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, Tübingen, Germany.
    Developed impedance and centroidal controllers for a torque-controlled biped, deployed variable-horizon MPC on the real biped Bolt (code), and contributed to actuator model identification for a one-legged robot.
